Interviewee S. booked two IPL tickets (about Rs 1,200 each, Rs 2,400 total) on BookMyShow for a match at the Ahmedabad stadium. Only physical tickets were available, delivered home. The match was moved to Chennai. Support had no call option; on chat, for the first 3 to 4 days they said they did not know what would happen. Then BookMyShow offered two options by WhatsApp and message: keep the ticket for a later match, or take a refund. The refund needed a Google form, and then the physical tickets had to reach the stadium for scanning, at S.'s own cost (Rs 150 by DTDC, about a week in transit). S. sent the courier photo to support. The refund came about 15 days after that, roughly 20 days in all. Convenience fee and GST were likely not refunded (transcript unclear).

When asked whether he would hand the whole process to an app that contacts, emails and messages for him, S. said yes, no objection. This was a yes/no question with no price attached, so it shows tolerance, not demand.

**Implication for build:** the pain is the not-knowing, the unexplained steps and the waiting, not the act of sending one message. The product must name the route, list the exact steps and proof, give a date, and come back.
