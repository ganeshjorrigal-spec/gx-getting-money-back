topic: why people lose stuck money
claim: Most stuck money is not refused, it is outlasted; people quit because chasing costs more than the refund.
type: insight
source: five customer conversations from Ganesh's earlier case-competition research
date: 2026-09-10
status: active
superseded-by: —
---
In five conversations, no company said no. One person blocked her card after emails went nowhere, one quit after a single draining call, one abandons Rs 2,000 to 4,000 a year, one recovered money only through a personal bank contact, one was billed after a free trial silently turned paid.

**Implication for build:** the product's core value is persistence (follow-ups and escalation on a clock), not a better first message.
