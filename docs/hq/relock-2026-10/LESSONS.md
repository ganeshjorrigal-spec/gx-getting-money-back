## Confirmed lessons
1. One bracket beats breadth. Every critic punished "three equal playbooks". The sheet's user, trigger, stories and landing must point at one person on one day. Ganesh's one-milestone build call can stand; the pitch is flights.
2. Flights lead because the law fixes both the date and the owner (CAR M-II 3(a), 3(c)); stores only have "a reasonable period". That is the defensible reason, not "bigger pain".
3. The ChatGPT-check answer must be shown, not claimed: the pinning question on one thread ("On which date did the airline pay you this refund?"), owner flipped by rule in code, a "Who did what" count on screen, and proof numbers (6 of 10 flight lines from Gemini were superseded; wrong MakeMyTrip grievance officer; 9 of 10 store lines unsupported).
4. "Outlasted, not refused" stays as the root insight; success by 17 Oct is replies read and cases moved, not money landed. Say so.
5. Do not overclaim the blame loop: the X sample does not quote it. Public proof: Bilaspur commission, Jul 2026 (Go Air paid Paytm; Paytm held it since a 2020 cancellation; Rs 6,437 plus Rs 15,000 ordered). REPORTED.
6.
