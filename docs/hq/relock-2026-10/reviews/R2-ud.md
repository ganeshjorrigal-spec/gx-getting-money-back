## The thing both drafts get wrong (read this before the per-draft notes)

**1. Your hero story is the least seen pattern in your data.** The loop both drafts sell, the demo, the share line, the "who owes you" flip, is "travel site blames airline, airline says it paid the travel site". R2-1 is honest that no post quotes the travel site saying "we are waiting for the airline", and the airline saying "already paid your agent" shows up in 0 of 29. The only hard record is one consumer commission order, about a 2020 flight, about Paytm.

What your data does show, again and again: "refund processed" and no money (7 of 29), and "please DM us" then silence (17 of 29). That is the common story. One common story first. Lead with it: "they say processed, my bank says nothing, nobody answers my DM". The agent gets the ARN or UTR and the date it left, from the right officer, in writing. The owner flip is story two, when it actually happens.

This also helps 17 Oct. Getting an ARN or a dated written answer from a grievance officer on an already overdue case can happen inside a week. A full owner flip on a real case probably will not.

**2. You are mixing up "a real moment" with "a live case".** Both drafts say the JTBD gets rewritten when the first live case arrives. You do not need a live case to get a real moment. You need one person who had a flight refund stuck in the last 12 months, resolved or given up, who will talk to you for 20 minutes. Ask your Isha groups, your Crucible batch and your ex-Performics friends that question tomorrow: "Has a flight refund ever got stuck for you, even if it came later?" That pool is far bigger than "stuck right now", and it gives you the JTBD in a human's words by Sun 11. Live cases then prove the product, not the moment.

**3. Replying to strangers on X about refunds looks exactly like a scam.** Fake "customer care" accounts reply to these complaint posts every day to steal OTPs and UPI PINs. Your first-10 plan starts with replying to those same posts. Neither draft deals with this. The trust answer has to be visible in the reply itself: no DM, no phone number, no link to click, "I will never ask for an OTP or bank details, you send the mail from your own Gmail", your real name and LinkedIn. And test the yes rate on the first 10 replies before you promise 40.

**4. "Nothing leaves your hands" is a little too clean.** The case inbox is in CC, so copies of the company replies, with PNR and booking ID, reach Tickback. Say that plainly: "Tickback sees only the mails you CC it on, nothing else, and never your login, OTP or bank." That is still a strong answer and it is true.

**5. Check whether 1915 is actually faster for these companies.** NCH works through convergence partners. If MakeMyTrip, Goibibo or the airlines are on that list, 1915 may be the fastest free route, and your "Tickback first, 1915 later" line weakens. I do not know if they are. Find out on Sat 10 (the NCH site lists partners) and write the answer down either way. If they are partners, your honest pitch becomes "Tickback gets you the written, dated thread and the right officer first, so when you call 1915 you have everything in hand". That is still a good line.

## R2-1 ("one bracket, shown not claimed")

**JTBD and core loop: does it read like a real moment in Ganesh's voice?**
Parts of it, yes. The push, the anxiety and the user flow story sound like you. The seven stories below them mostly do not; they read like a product spec written in first person.

- Best line: "Somebody says it is with the airline, but the airline I never even wrote to." That is a real feeling, in your voice. Close second: "so I just kept checking the bank every morning, and I think slowly I was giving up on it."
- Worst line: story 3, "For MakeMyTrip or Goibibo, their own terms say they try to pass refunds on within 24 hours of getting them from the airline, so the next mail quotes the airline's reply and that line, and asks for the ARN or UTR." No passenger thinks this sentence. Also weak: story 4, "Sometimes the honest answer is that the other side is right", which sounds like a writer, not a passenger. And rule numbers like "(3(c))" and "(3(f))" inside a user story break the voice every time.

**First-10 plan: concrete and doable from Sat 10?**
Mostly yes, and the best I have seen from you. Dated channels, a target per channel, a definition of a user (sent at least one message), a daily count, a checkpoint on Wed 14 at 18:00, and a switch rule decided in advance. The X channel meets the person at the exact trigger moment, which is smart.

What is missing or shaky:
- The scam problem above. Write the actual first X reply into the plan, word for word, and show it to two friends to ask "would you trust this?"
- The 13 flight posts in your sample include 2 international (out of scope) and some are weeks or months old. Expect a few to be already solved or dead. Fresh daily search matters more than the backlog.
- Channel targets (5 to 6 from X, 2 from Isha, and so on) have no basis yet. Fine as guesses, but call them guesses.
- What happens after a yes? The user has to share the cancellation mail with you. If that goes over DM, you are doing the thing you criticise. Say where it goes (email to a Tickback address) and that your log keeps counts only, no names or PNRs.
- Sun 11, Crucible demo: on Sun 11 the only demo live is events. Showing an event demo while asking for flight cases will confuse people. Show the hand-written flight mail instead, or wait for Tue.

**Hand-waved or overclaimed**
- "Commissions now pin the middle company." It is one commission, one order. Say "one commission".
- "Nothing leaves your hands" (see point 4).
- The "Why me" paragraph about Gemini's errors is true and labelled, but it is long and technical. One sentence is enough: a strong research model got most of the current rules wrong; my playbook is checked line by line against the DGCA text.
- Length. The paste part is far too long for a lock sheet. Every extra paragraph is another place to get caught. Honest, but heavy.

**What I would tell Ganesh to change**
1. Get one real person's flight refund story by Sun 11 (past case is fine) and rewrite the JTBD from it.
2. Lead the core loop with "processed but no money, DM then silence"; make the owner flip story two.
3. Strip clause numbers out of the user stories; keep them in the "how the dates work" block.
