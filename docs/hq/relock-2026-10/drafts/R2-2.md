## PRODUCT

**Onboarding:** no sign-up. Paste the mail, add PNR; in a minute see who owes, by when, which rule, who sits above. No case? "Try a demo": a labelled travel site and airline reply in your Gmail thread, then a "Who did what" count.

**The core loop (user stories):** (from public posts until my first real case, by Sun 11)
When my flight got cancelled and the app is showing "refund initiated" since many days, I want to know who is actually having my money and by which date, so that I can stop running between two companies.
Push: same bot template, "please DM us", then nothing. Anxiety: not a Swiggy-sized amount, and every blog is saying different days.
1. I paste the mail: travel-site booking, so the airline owes in 14 working days. Nothing to send yet, honestly a relief.
2. Date passes; the site says "waiting for the airline". A mail to the airline's Nodal Officer is ready, site in CC, one question: on which date did you pay the site? I tap send.
3. The airline says it paid last week. Now it looks clear who has my money; the next mail quotes MakeMyTrip's 24-hour terms and asks for the UTR.
4. Still nothing: Appellate Authority, then an AirSewa or 1915 complaint from the dated thread.

**The AI-first part:**
Research plus verification is the product. Building it, Gemini Deep Research took 6 of 10 load-bearing lines from the superseded rule (21 working days; Rev 3 says 14) and gave Yatra's investor-relations officer as a passenger contact. My verifier caught it at source (OBSERVATION). Each playbook is researched once, verified, stored as data.
In a case, the model reads each reply (who it points at, what it claims, any date); code flips the owner by rule and sets dates. ChatGPT never sees the airline's reply on your thread, or comes back.
