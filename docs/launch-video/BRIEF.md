# Refund Genie launch video: mission brief (shared by every agent)

## Mission
Write the launch video for Refund Genie that makes a real Indian flyer with a stuck flight refund stop scrolling, feel seen, believe there is now a better way, and open refundgenie.app. Output: 3 top scripts with storyboards that Ganesh (a non-filmmaker MBA student, solo) can actually produce this week with AI video tools (Higgsfield, Google Flow / Veo), plus phone screen recording of the real app, plus himself on camera if a script needs it.

## Fixed decisions (from Ganesh, do not change)
- Audience: real Indian flyers (not judges). Platform: Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / X, vertical 9:16, sound on but must also work muted with captions.
- Language heard: Hindi (natural spoken Hindi; everyday English words that Indians actually say in Hindi are fine: refund, booking, flight, app, mail). On-screen captions: Hindi in Devanagari plus a small English subtitle line.
- Claims: show ONLY what is live today (see "What you may claim"). No AI phone calls in the video at all.
- Length: you choose, but 30 to 60 seconds; shorter wins if it carries the same feeling.
- Production: open (AI-generated scenes, real app screen recording, Ganesh on camera, or a mix).

## The product in one line
Refund Genie is an AI agent for domestic flight refunds in India that are past their date. It tells you who actually has your money, when it was due under DGCA rules, writes every mail to the right officer, reads the replies, switches who to chase when they blame each other, and keeps coming back on the right date until the money lands.

## What you may claim (live today)
- Paste the cancellation mail or a screenshot. No sign-up. No login to your airline or travel app. Never asks for OTP, card or bank details.
- It tells you: who owes you (airline or travel app), the date it was due under the DGCA rule, which officer sits above them.
- It writes every mail and every follow-up, to the right named officer (Nodal Officer, grievance officer), and you send it from your own Gmail with Genie's case inbox in CC. (Genie sending mails itself after your written permission is built but not yet open for real companies: do not make "Genie sends it for you" the core promise; "Genie likhta hai, aap bas send dabao" is safe.)
- It reads the replies and tells you the next step; when the travel app says "airline se poocho" and the airline says "humne bhej diya", Genie switches whom to chase.
- It comes back on the date (calendar reminder), so you do not have to remember.
- You never re-explain the story: one case, one thread, all history in one place.
- Price: free until 3 of your refunds land, then Rs 49 a year. Safe line: "Free hai." (optional "Rs 49 saal ka, baad mein").
- URL: refundgenie.app

## What you must NOT do
- No AI phone calls, no "Genie calls the airline", no voice agent.
- No promise of a guaranteed refund or a timeline ("7 din mein paisa wapas" is forbidden). No "legal action", no "we sue".
- Do not name or show real airlines or travel apps (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Cleartrip, EaseMyTrip), their logos, uniforms or app screens. Use fictional or generic ones ("Airline", "travel app", a made-up name like "UdanTrip"). Do not mock a real brand.
- No copyrighted characters or recognisable film scenes recreated; no song lyrics; no impersonating real celebrities. Cultural references must be tropes and archetypes Indians recognise (IVR hold music feel, "aapki call humare liye mahatvapurn hai" style lines, joint-family dinner, wedding season, Diwali trip, bank SMS ping, chai tapri, 90s Doordarshan feel, Ramlila, Indian ad tropes like Fevicol-style exaggeration) written as original work.
- No real person's name or quote (Rohan has not approved his quote). A fictional character inspired by real patterns is fine.
- No em dashes in anything Ganesh will read.

## Evidence ledger (what the pain really is)
- OBSERVATION (Ganesh's tester Rohan, 11 Oct): booked a return flight on a travel app (~Rs 15,000), cancelled within 24h. Airline said the app would pass the refund; the app wanted proof he did not have. 7 to 8 calls over 3 weeks, a new person each time, retelling the whole story every time. His words (paraphrase, do not quote): writing the mail is not the problem, the constant follow-up is; "just get me my refund".
- OBSERVATION (5 earlier conversations): nobody was refused. Money is OUTLASTED, not refused. People quit because chasing costs more than the refund. One blocked her card, one quit after one draining call, one abandons Rs 2,000 to 4,000 a year, one got it only via a personal bank contact.
- OBSERVATION (IPL ticket case): pain is the not-knowing, the unexplained steps, the waiting; ~20 days; self-paid courier; silence.
- REPORTED (29 public X complaints): "Please DM us" is the standard first reply (17 of 29), then silence. "Refund processed" but no money in bank (7). Travel site says "waiting for the airline" (4 of 15 flight posts); the blame loop. Chatbots repeating the same template (3). Waits from 1 to 120 days, median about 20; SpiceJet-via-travel-site cases at 90 to 120 days.
- VERIFIED: DGCA rule (revised Feb 2026): for travel-site bookings, the airline is responsible and the refund is due in 14 working days. Old numbers (21 days) still circulate on blogs. Each airline and travel site must name a Nodal Officer / grievance officer.
- INFERENCE: the emotional core is not anger at one company; it is the humiliation and fatigue of being the unpaid messenger between two companies, the feeling that persistence is the only currency and you are running out of it, and a quiet resignation ("chhodo, jaane do").
- ASSUMPTION: Indian viewers respond strongly to exaggerated, warm, family-and-humour storytelling (Fevicol, Cadbury, Ceat, Fevikwik, Center Fresh, Cred's "not everyone gets it") and to precise, shared micro-details (the hold music, the bank SMS, "5-7 working days").

## Video-craft lessons from Ganesh's class (apply them)
- Hook in the first 1 to 3 seconds: turning point, insane outcome, or relatable pain; visual hooks (weird shot, aesthetic shot, snap cuts); audio hooks (sudden sound cue, trending-style SFX like a comic "faaah").
- Three layers: External (what it does), Emotional (why it matters to a person), Philosophical (what the world gets wrong). Two done well is enough. They can be shown, not said.
- Formats: talking head, skit/story, VO + visuals, visuals only.
- Reference wins: "Diet Claude" skit (relatable obsession + jarring error sound + hero entrance + one-line tagline); "Shram" (montage of real "sorry" messages + calm visuals + "Never be sorry again"); "Yours Only" (the surprise reveal of 10 identical wine bottles + sound flip).
- The teacher's extra note: be really creative; many cultural references can carry this.
- The name "Refund Genie" invites a mascot / magical sidekick, but a genie can feel gimmicky or untrustworthy for money; use it with care if at all.

## Production reality (AI video in Oct 2026, solo creator)
- AI clips are 5 to 10 seconds each; keep a single character consistent with a reference image; fewer characters and fewer locations is safer.
- AI lip-sync in Hindi is improving but still risky; voiceover over shots, or characters seen from behind / phone screens / reactions without dialogue, is safer. On-screen text inside AI video is unreliable: add captions and UI text in editing.
- The real app can be screen-recorded on a phone (light warm "receipt / calm ledger" look, serif headings, amber for overdue, green only for money received).
- Ganesh can record his own voice for VO, or use an AI Hindi voice.
- Budget: a few hours of generation, CapCut-level editing.

## Scoring rubric (each 1 to 5, with evidence; total /50)
1. Hook (first 3 s): would a flyer scrolling Reels stop? Name the exact frame/sound.
2. Pain truth: does it show the real pain from the ledger (outlasted, blame loop, re-explaining, "refund initiated" with no money, DM-us silence) rather than generic "customer care is bad"?
3. Emotional pull: does a viewer feel something specific (recognition, laughter, relief, small rage) and attach to a person?
4. Product clarity: after one watch, can the viewer say what Genie does in one sentence, and is it shown not just told?
5. Honesty: zero forbidden claims; everything shown is live today; no real brands.
6. Cultural resonance: does it feel unmistakably Indian and original, not a translated Western ad, without leaning on copyrighted material?
7. Memorability: a line, image or sound people would repeat or share.
8. Producibility: can Ganesh make it this week with AI video + screen recording + editing, at good quality? Flag shots AI will fail at.
9. Hindi quality: natural, speakable, short lines, works with captions.
10. CTA: clear next action (refundgenie.app), low effort, trust cue (no OTP / no login / free).
Pass threshold for a finalist: total >= 40 and no score below 3, and Honesty must be 5.
