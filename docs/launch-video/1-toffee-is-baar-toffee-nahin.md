## A. The idea in one paragraph

Every Hindi-speaking Indian has stood at a kirana counter, heard "छुट्टा नहीं है, टॉफ़ी ले लो", and taken the toffee, because asking for ₹1 feels petty and the bhaiya is a friend. That small, warm ritual is how people lose flight refunds: they accept a message in place of money ("Refund initiated", "5-7 working days", "आप शुरू से बताइए", "airline से पूछिए") until they close their fist and let ₹5,400 go. The evidence ledger backs every beat: in 5 conversations nobody was refused, the money was outlasted (OBSERVATION); the tester's pain was retelling and the constant follow-up, not writing the mail (OBSERVATION, 11 Oct); 7 of 29 public complaints were "refund processed" with no money, and the "waiting for the airline" blame loop showed up in 4 of 15 flight posts (REPORTED); the DGCA rule puts travel-site refunds on the airline within 14 working days, so the stuck feeling starts after about day 21 (VERIFIED). The film's counter runs to day 30, matching the sample case's "Overdue by about 7 working days". One object does every job: the toffee is the hook, the pain, the blame loop and the giving up. The ending answers it with the other half of the kirana ritual, the khata: a debt that is written down is a debt that is remembered. Refund Genie is the khata for your refund: who owes, by when, what they replied, the next mail written. It is honest by construction: toffees keep arriving after Refund Genie, nothing stops the companies, and the person presses Send himself.

## D2. Production in Higgsfield / Google Flow (mostly-AI route)

**Short answer.** The app section must be a real screen recording on either route (lesson 16: never mock the app). Everything at the counter can be AI, but hands holding wrapped toffees, a pen writing Devanagari, and any printed text are where AI fails. The best mix: AI for the face, the pour, the drops, the lone toffee and the drawer; live phone for the khata insert (5 minutes, one hand, one pen). A fully AI version is possible but expect to spend 10 or more hours generating (lesson 10).

**What AI will do badly (plan around it)**
- Hands: six fingers, fingers merging with toffees, palms that change shape between frames. Keep the palm still, use only 1 to 1.5 s slices, generate 4 to 6 variants per shot, and pick takes where the wrist is cropped.
- Wrappers: AI invents fake printed text and logo-like marks on wrappers, sometimes close to real brands. Ask for plain single-colour cellophane, avoid purple and gold, check every frame.
