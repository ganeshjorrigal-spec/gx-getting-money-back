## Hard rules learned (now part of the brief)
1. AGENCY HONESTY (failed in 8 of 9). Genie must never be shown or said to chase, stop, knock, send, beat or override companies on its own. What is true and live: Genie reads, figures out, writes, remembers, says whom to ask next, and has the next mail ready on the date. The PERSON presses Send in their own Gmail. Show that tap once, on screen. Safe lines: "Genie लिखता है, आप बस send दबाओ", "अगला mail तैयार", "पीछा याद Genie रखता है". Unsafe: "Genie पीछा करता है", "Genie नहीं थकता" (alone), "हम नहीं थकते" (alone), freezes or whistles that stop the companies.
2. NO MONEY-LANDED ENDING. No "credited" bank SMS, no "+Rs" counter, no counter resetting to zero as if solved, no "solved by next Sunday". End on relief, on the person's time given back, or on the next mail ready. The win is "you stop running", not "money guaranteed".
3. EFFECT, NOT INTENT. Do not say companies wait on purpose or that it is their plan. Evidence shows the effect (people give up), not intent. Safe: "मना नहीं किया। बस आप थक गए।", "थका देना ही काफ़ी है।", "जो थक गया, उसका पैसा वहीं रह गया।"
4. BLAME LOOP STAGED CORRECTLY. Who owes (responsible) is not the same as who holds the money. Card wording: "Who owes you: Airline" (the app's real label), never "money is with the airline". Correct switch: first mail to the travel app's grievance officer; reply "waiting for the airline" or "airline से पूछिए"; Genie then points to the airline's Nodal Officer with the thread. Never have the reply contradict the card.
5. APP SECTION: MAX 3 BEATS, readable, labelled. (a) paste the cancellation mail or screenshot, see "Who owes you" + "Overdue" + due date; (b) the mail is written, tap "Open in Gmail", press Send in Gmail; (c) a reply comes or the date comes, the next mail is ready (calendar event, not app push). Add a small "Sample case" tag. Hold each beat at least 3 s. The real app UI is in ENGLISH (labels "Who owes you", "Overdue", "Due about ...", "Open in Gmail"); add Hindi captions over it, do not invent Hindi UI.
6. SCREEN RECORDING SOURCE. Record a made-up case using the app's own fictional names (Demo Air, DemoTrips) or crop/blur any real airline name. No real brand on screen. Record only the normal case view where the user taps "Open in Gmail" and sends. Never record the public flight demo (/start/?demo=flight: it shows Genie sending mails itself and a simulated call) or the homepage example (it names real brands).
7. MUTED-FIRST HOOK. Big burned-in Devanagari caption on the hook. Use the most universal micro-details: "refund initiated", "5-7 working days", "debited, never credited", "आप शुरू से बताइए". "Please DM us" is narrower (only people who complain on X).
8. DAY COUNTERS: consistent and plausible. 14 working days is about 20 calendar days, so stuck feeling starts around day 21+. Do not show chasing on day 8.
9. NAMES: no "UdanTrip" (sounds like Udaan and the govt UDAN scheme). Use "Airline" and "Travel App" generically, or the app's own "Demo Air" / "DemoTrips". Do not use Rs 15,000 (it is the tester's real amount); use another amount such as Rs 12,400 or Rs 18,600.
10. PRODUCIBILITY. No multi-person lip-sync, no synced choruses, max 2 locations, max 1 consistent AI character. Real props and hands shot on a phone beat AI for stamps, drawings, notes, hands, text. Fantastical elements are where AI earns its place. Plan 10+ hours of generation for any AI-heavy cut; prefer 4 to 6.
11. HINDI PACE: about 2.5 spoken words per second maximum. Long VO lines go to captions. Spell the name जीनी. Avoid English subtitle "Let it go" (Frozen).
12. FOUNDER: saying "my own refund never got stuck" kills credibility with flyers. Use second person ("आपने कितनी बार...") instead of a founder bio.
13. DEMO/SAMPLE label on any staged case. Free is fine; Rs 49 optional. CTA must hold the URL 4 s, ideally spoken.
14. CULTURE: references must be instantly known to 25 to 40 year olds in Hindi India; avoid ones that echo a specific TV serial's look (Betaal 1985) or sacred objects used negatively (drooping tulsi).

## Round 2 lessons
15. THE "WHO OWES vs FIRST MAIL" GAP (flagged in 5 of 6). The card says "Who owes you: Airline" but the first mail goes to the travel app. Muted viewers read that as a contradiction. Always caption beat (b): "पहला mail: जहाँ booking की थी (Airline CC में)". This matches the app: the first mail goes to the travel site's grievance officer with the airline's Nodal Officer in CC.
16. RECORD, DON'T MOCK. Calendar event wording, the reply-read screen and "next mail ready" must be recorded from the live app. Never build those screens in CapCut. Blur the Gmail To: field (test address). Keep the "Sample case" tag on every app frame, including payoff frames.
17. A FACE IS NEEDED. Hands-only or back-only films score 3 on emotion. Show the person's face at least twice, at the low point and at the relief.
18. FRAME 0 MUST SAY "FLIGHT". Show a cancelled e-ticket ("DEL → BLR · CANCELLED", no airline name) or a boarding-pass prop from the first frame, or the film reads as UPI, kirana or a generic ad.
19. VALUE = MEMORY OF THE FOLLOW-UP, NOT "WRITES A MAIL". "I can write a mail too" is the flyer's objection. Lead the product claim with "पीछा याद जीनी रखता है। अगला mail तैयार।"
20. CULTURAL MECHANICS MUST BE RIGHT. In kho-kho the runner gives the kho to a sitter. Get the game's rules right, and get the joke out of breaking them.
21. TIME SPAN. Long season spans (monsoon to winter) say "it still takes months". Keep the after-Genie span short.
22. SAY "Refund Genie" ALOUD at least once.

## Round 3 lessons
23. A MADE-UP COMPANY ON THE NORMAL FLOW IS AN UNKNOWN COMPANY (verified in code). flightContact() only returns VERIFIED real contacts (lib/flight-kb.ts:463). Demo role addresses exist only on demo cases (lib/flight.ts:107), and the demo shows Genie sending by itself. So a Demo Air / DemoTrips case on the normal view shows: To "Paste the address from the company's own page", no airline in CC, "Who to contact next: No mail needed yet", and after a reply "We don't hold a checked address for Demo Air". FIX: a small Codex change, a "Sample case" mode on the normal flow (user still taps Open in Gmail and Send) that uses labelled sample role addresses. Until it ships, no app beat is recorded. Prompt: docs/hq/codex-prompts/2026-10-12-sample-case-for-video.md.
24. SCRIPT THE APP FROM THE CODE, THEN FROM SCREENSHOTS. Run the exact fake case once, screenshot every frame, caption only from those screenshots. Shared spec: APP-BEATS.md. All three scripts use it.
25. EXACT LABELS (confirmed): "Who owes you", "⚠ Overdue", "Overdue by about N working days", "Due [date]" (never "Due about"), the card line "You booked on a travel site, so the airline is responsible for your refund.", "Your next mail", "Open in Gmail", "Yes, I've sent it", "New reply from", "Your check-in: [date]", "Download one-time calendar reminder", event title "Refund Genie refund check-in", "They replied?" paste box.
26. "NEW REPLY FROM" ONLY COMES THROUGH THE CASE INBOX, never from the paste box (convex/cases.ts:92). The inbox watch starts after the tap "Yes, I've sent it" and checks about every 10 minutes. An unknown sender may add a "Does that look right?" confirm step first (lib/flight.ts:78): record it, jump-cut it.
27. CROP JUST BELOW "Open in Gmail". "Let Genie send and follow up" sits right under it in the same card (app/c/flight-case-view.tsx:147), plus the line "...give Genie written permission above" (:151). Never show either. Also crop "So Ganesh can follow up".
28. ONE STATE PER CUT. The check-in card sits below the whole draft card (:159); it cannot share a frame with the next mail. Each app state gets its own cut, held 3 s. Max 4 states in the whole app section.
29. CALENDAR IS A ONE-TIME FILE, NOT A REMINDER SERVICE. Say "Check-in की तारीख़ आपके calendar में", never "जीनी याद दिलाएगा". No phone lighting up by itself with a "ting" (reads as a push alert; the app has none).
30. "पीछा याद रखता है" reads as Genie chasing. Use "follow-up कब और किससे: Refund Genie याद रखता है" or "अगला mail तैयार". Screenshots are UPLOADED ("screenshot डालो"), mail text is PASTED.
31. RECORDING HYGIENE: fresh browser profile (the paywall counts landed cases per device); a sample mail that states every fact, so answer chips naming real brands never appear; never show the homepage (names real airlines).
