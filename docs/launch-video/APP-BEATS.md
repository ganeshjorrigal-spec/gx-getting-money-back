## Exact on-screen labels (confirmed in code)
"Who owes you" · "⚠ Overdue" · "Overdue by about N working days" · "Due [date]" (never "Due about") · "You booked on a travel site, so the airline is responsible for your refund." · "Your next mail" · "Open in Gmail" · "Yes, I've sent it" · "New reply from ..." · "Your check-in: [date]" · "Download one-time calendar reminder" · calendar event title "Refund Genie refund check-in".

## The beats (max 4 app states, one state per cut, each held at least 3 s)
- A. THE CARD (3 to 4 s). "Who owes you: Demo Air", "⚠ Overdue", "Due [date]", and the line "You booked on a travel site, so the airline is responsible for your refund." Highlight box (shape only) around Who owes you and Overdue. Caption idea: "किसकी ज़िम्मेदारी, कब तक: rule से साफ़।"
- B. YOU PRESS SEND (4 to 5 s, two cuts). Cut 1: "Your next mail" with To (DemoTrips grievance, role readable) and CC (Demo Air Nodal Officer, role readable), crop just below "Open in Gmail", tap. Cut 2: Gmail compose, press Send. Captions: "पहला mail: जहाँ booking की थी (Airline CC में)" then "Mail जीनी लिखता है। Send आप दबाते हो।"
- C. REPLY READ, NEXT MAIL READY (3 to 4 s). "New reply from DemoTrips" with its one-line summary (pending with the airline), then (separate cut if needed) the next mail To the Demo Air Nodal Officer. Caption: "जवाब पढ़ लिया। अगला mail: अब Airline को, तैयार।"
- D. OPTIONAL CHECK-IN (2 to 3 s). "Your check-in: [date]" or the phone calendar event "Refund Genie refund check-in". Caption: "Check-in की तारीख़ आपके calendar में।"

## Safe product lines (use these, not variants)
- "follow-up कब और किससे: Refund Genie याद रखता है।" (never "पीछा जीनी करता है", avoid "पीछा याद रखता है")
- "Mail जीनी लिखता है। Send आप दबाते हो।" / "आप बस send दबाओ।"
- "जवाब आया तो पढ़कर बताता है, अब किसे लिखना है।"
- "अगला mail तैयार।"
- "Cancellation mail paste करो, या screenshot डालो।"
- "Free है। No sign-up. OTP कभी नहीं।"

## Never on screen
"Let Genie send and follow up", "give Genie written permission", any call or "Genie is on it" chase line, "Who has your money now", "It's in" / "Money landed", the paywall card, the browser address bar, real email addresses (blur the domain part only, keep role names readable), a phone lighting up by itself.
