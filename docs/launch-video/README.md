## Before any app scene is recorded
All three record the app ONLY from an unlinked sample case. Codex must ship it first: `docs/hq/codex-prompts/2026-10-12-sample-case-for-video.md`. App captions stay provisional until Codex's screenshots arrive (see `APP-BEATS.md`). The live scenes can be shot now.

## How these were made
9 concepts in round 1, each reviewed by an independent adversarial critic; the best 6 revised and re-reviewed; 3 finals reviewed again with every app claim checked against the code; fixed; then a fresh final judge.
