## 3. Drop-off and doubt points

**Viewer 1 (Delhi, stuck 40 days, muted, 11 pm)**
- 0:01 to 0:03: if the potli looks like a craft-class puppet (flat light, wobbly turn), she reads a kids' ad and swipes. The caption and the "REFUND INITIATED" tag hold her.
- 0:14.5 (6a): one illegible second of an archive swipe.
- 0:22.5: a phone glows by itself. "So it notifies me?" It doesn't.
- 0:28 to 0:37: dense screens, two captions each. She can't read them in time. This is the biggest scroll-away risk.

**Viewer 2 (GrowthX reviewer)**
- 0:22.5: a hard pivot from metaphor to product. The owner never chooses to act, so the rescue feels unearned.
- 0:28: "Airline CC में" over a blurred CC proves nothing.

**Viewer 3 (solo shooter, 2 days)**
- Day 0: the Codex contact change is a blocker the script treats as optional.
- The corridor: few societies let you switch the tube light, and LED tubes don't flicker.
- Beat (c) needs a real inbound mail and a 10-minute poll. Allow an hour.

**Potli:** charming for 25 to 40 only if the voice is adult and sincere (the script's direction is right) and the object reads as a real money potli: velvet or silk, zari edge, stuffed round. A thin ₹80 cotton potli under a cold overhead tube looks limp, its buttons get no catchlight, and it reads as childish. Fixable (MUST-FIX 7).

**Hindi:** natural overall. "पीछा याद ... रखता है" sounds slightly translated; this audience says "follow-up". "अब जाने मत दो" lands as a bookend but is abstract on mute. No guarantee language anywhere.

## 4. MUST-FIX

1. **Gate the recording on test contacts.** Delete "If Ganesh creates it himself, paste this made-up mail". Replace with: "Do not record until Codex has added allow-listed test contacts for Demo Air (Nodal Officer) and DemoTrips (Grievance Officer) that the normal flow resolves. Then confirm three things on screen: To = DemoTrips grievance, CC = Demo Air nodal, and 'Who to contact next' is not 'No mail needed yet'." Flag in "For HQ": this is a code change, and it must not mark fake entries as real VERIFIED sources visible to users.
2. **Beat (c) reply path.** Replace step 3 with: "After pressing Send in Gmail, tap 'Yes, I've sent it' (off camera). Reply-all from the DemoTrips test account and keep the case code in the subject. Wait up to 30 min for the poll. Never use the 'They replied?' paste box for this shot; it does not create the 'New reply from' card."
3. **Make the CC proof readable.** Use test addresses whose local part says the role, such as `nodal.demoair@...` and `grievance.demotrips@...`. Blur only the domain. Label: "पहला mail: जहाँ booking की थी (Airline CC में)".
4. **Beat (c) hold times.** Three states in 5 s breaks the 3 s hold rule. Cut 6a (1 s) and trim shot 3 to 2.5 s, which frees 1.5 s. Beat (c) becomes 6.5 s: reply card with "still Demo Air" 3 s, next mail 2 s, calendar event 1.5 s. Or move the calendar event onto the phone screen in 12b.
5. **Shot 8.** Replace it with: "Dark corridor. The owner's hand (same T-shirt as 6b) picks up his phone beside the potli and opens refundgenie.app/start. The screen shows the paste box (never the homepage, which names real brands)." Drop the self-lit "ting". The owner chooses to act, so the pivot is earned.
6. **Beat (b) density.** Split it into 2.5 s on the mail with the CC label, then 2.5 s of Gmail Send with "जीनी लिखता है। आप बस send दबाओ।" Take 0.5 s from the end-card chips. Crop so the "Let Genie send and follow up" card and the line "...give Genie written permission above." never enter the frame.
7. **Potli look test, pass or fail.** Buy velvet or silk with a zari border. Fill it firmly with rice so it is round. Steam out the creases. Add a warm side lamp as a key light at 45 degrees so each button gets a catchlight; the tube light is only a backlight. Pass test: three friends see it on a phone at arm's length and none says "bachchon wala". Fail means the hybrid AI route.
