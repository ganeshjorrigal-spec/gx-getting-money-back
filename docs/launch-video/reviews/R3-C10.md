## 3. Drop-off and doubt points

**Viewer 1, Delhi flyer, muted, 11 pm**
- 0.0 to 2.4 s: the A6 ticket is unreadable on a phone, so it looks like a kirana or UPI-nostalgia reel. **Highest drop risk.**
- 2.4 to 3.4 s: seven caption words over a pour for 1.0 s: unreadable.
- 17.4 to 21.0 s: the turn lands. The mapping is clean: the shop owes you change and hands you a substitute; the company owes you money and hands you a message.
- 38.6 to 41.0 s: a polite slide-back usually means "रहने दो भैया", which is giving up the ₹1: the same resignation as shot 8.

**Viewer 2, GrowthX reviewer**
- 21.0 s: "पीछा याद रखेगा Refund Genie" sounds like Genie does the chasing, while the screen shows you doing it.
- 26.8 to 38.6 s: an empty To field or "No mail needed yet" on screen means the demo contradicts the voice-over.
- 35.4 s: "तारीख़ जीनी याद रखता है" invites the question "does it remind me?" The honest answer is a one-time calendar file.

**Viewer 3, solo producer, 2 days**
- Day 1 recording session: the "check before shooting" list fails on items 3, 5 and 6, with no plan B written. He will be stuck the night before the shoot.
- Edit: six tags pinned to toffees in a moving palm need tracking.

## 4. MUST-FIX (by impact)

1. **Demo companies have no contacts on the normal flow.** Get an HQ decision before the shoot: Codex adds sample-only Demo Air/DemoTrips contacts pointing to test addresses (HQ-owned change), or the script follows the app: in beat (b), Ganesh types test Gmail B into To and it is blurred. Caption becomes "पहला mail: जहाँ booking की थी" (drop "Airline CC में"). Beat (c) caption becomes "अगला mail: अब Airline को · तैयार". Beat (a) is shown as two crops joined by a jump cut ("Who owes you / Demo Air", then "⚠ Overdue / Due [date]"), so "No mail needed yet" never appears.
2. **Beat (c) reply path.** A pasted reply never shows "New reply from". Use the case inbox (tracked reply). Record the "Refund Genie read: … Does that look right?" card, tap "Looks right" (jump cut), then the "New reply from DemoTrips" card. If tracking fails, use the "Refund Genie read: [summary]" card as beat (c) with the same caption "जवाब आया: Airline के पास pending है".
3. **Split beat (c) into two cuts.** 35.4 to 37.0 s: top of "Your next mail". 37.0 to 38.6 s: the "Your check-in: [date]" card, or the phone calendar event "Refund Genie refund check-in". It cannot be one frame.
4. **Slide-back cultural mechanic.** Shot 14 change: Ganesh slides the toffee back, and the shopkeeper's hand pulls out a small khata and writes "बाकी ₹1". No money changes hands, the debt is now remembered, and it mirrors what Genie does. Keep the line "इस बार, टॉफ़ी नहीं।"
5. **Muted hook.** Print the ticket at A5 with a red "CANCELLED" stamp about 3 cm tall. Bring caption 2 in at 1.4 s and hold it to 3.4 s: "₹12,400 का flight refund भी?" / "Even a ₹12,400 flight refund?"
6. **Turn line needs its noun.** Spoken: "एक रुपये पे टॉफ़ी चलती है। बारह हज़ार पे नहीं।" Extend shot 10 to 4.0 s (10 words). The film becomes 45.4 s.
7. **Agency and reminder wording.** Replace "पीछा याद रखेगा Refund Genie।" with "Follow-up कब, किससे: याद Refund Genie रखेगा।" Replace the spoken "तारीख़ जीनी याद रखता है" with "Check-in की तारीख़, आपके calendar में।" and use the calendar-event cut from fix 3.
8. **Recording hygiene.** Use a fresh browser profile (avoids the device paywall). Write the sample mail so the extractor gets every fact without asking: "Demo Air cancelled your domestic flight DEL to BLR, booked through DemoTrips, paid Rs 12,400 by UPI, PNR DEMO12, cancelled on [date]". That way no real-brand chips appear. In beat (b), scroll so "Open in Gmail" is the last element on screen, keeping the "Let Genie send and follow up" block below the crop.

## 6. New lesson

23. A FICTIONAL COMPANY IS AN UNKNOWN COMPANY. On the normal flow, Demo Air and DemoTrips have no verified contacts. The app then shows an empty To field, no airline in CC and "No mail needed yet", and a reply from an unknown sender triggers a "Does that look right?" step.
