## 3. Drop-off and doubt points

**Viewer 1 (Delhi, IndiGo via an app, 40 days, muted, 11 pm)**
- 0:06.5: two captions in 3.5 s; "हाँ जी, booking..." means nothing on mute.
- 0:20.5: biggest risk. Small English UI after 20 s of skit. "No mail needed yet" or "Paste the address" on screen reads as broken.
- 0:29.8: "जीनी लिखता है" comes before the memory payoff: "I have mailed them 5 times already."
- 0:40: everything says "Demo Air", so she cannot tell if it works for her airline.

**Viewer 2 (GrowthX reviewer)**
- 0:18: who blows the whistle, and why does only his head lift? It is never set up.
- 0:24: he walks off before sending anything, so the order feels slightly off.
- 0:32.8: the calendar tap lands on a case that already has a reply. A pasted reply makes no calendar event, so anyone who tries the app will spot the staging.

**Viewer 3 (solo student, 2 days, phone)**
- The live shoot is fine; the 50-minute golden window is tight but planned.
- From shot 7 the plan breaks on recording day: no To, no CC, a disabled button, a Calendar OAuth screen, and a case inbox that may be off for his test account.
