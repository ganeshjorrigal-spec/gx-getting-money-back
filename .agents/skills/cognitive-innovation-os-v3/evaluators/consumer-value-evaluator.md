# Evaluator — Consumer/User Value

## Questions

1. What meaningful progress does this enable?
2. In what situation is the value felt?
3. Is the benefit immediate, credible, and important enough to change behavior?
4. What existing workaround must it beat?
5. What friction, risk, trust, identity, or habit barrier remains?
6. Does the concept create value or merely demand attention?
7. Who may be excluded or harmed?

## Rating

- 5 — solves a high-value tension with a plausible adoption pathway.
- 4 — strong value; manageable barriers.
- 3 — useful but weakly differentiated or context-limited.
- 2 — interesting idea with unclear user progress.
- 1 — creator-centered, not user-centered.
