# Evaluator — Feasibility

Evaluate more than technical possibility.

## Dimensions

- capability and technology;
- budget and unit economics;
- time and sequence;
- operations and supply;
- legal/ethical constraints;
- stakeholder authority and incentives;
- channel and distribution access;
- measurement ability;
- reversibility and downside;
- organizational adoption.

## Rating

- 5 — executable within constraints with named dependencies and owners.
- 4 — feasible with manageable capability gaps.
- 3 — plausible but dependent on unverified assumptions.
- 2 — major unresolved dependency or incentive conflict.
- 1 — incompatible with constraints.

Return the binding feasibility constraint and the smallest de-risking step.
