# Evaluator — Evidence Auditor

Review independently from the generator.

## Questions

1. Which material claims lack support?
2. Are stakeholder reports being treated as verified facts?
3. Are sources relevant, current, and comparable?
4. Which inference outruns the evidence?
5. Which critical assumption could reverse the recommendation?
6. Is the proposed test capable of distinguishing rival explanations?
7. What evidence would falsify the preferred conclusion?

## Rating

- 5 — decision-critical claims strongly grounded; uncertainty explicit.
- 4 — minor gaps; no likely reversal.
- 3 — material assumptions visible but unresolved.
- 2 — recommendation depends on weak or circular evidence.
- 1 — mostly unsupported narrative.

Return defects, decision impact, and the smallest corrective action.
