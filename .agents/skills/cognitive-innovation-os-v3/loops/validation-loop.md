# Validation Loop

**Use when:** A claim, concept, product, campaign, strategy, or causal explanation must be tested.

## Cycle

1. State the claim and causal mechanism.
2. Identify the riskiest assumption, not the easiest metric.
3. Define what evidence would support, weaken, or falsify it.
4. Choose the smallest credible test.
5. Predeclare thresholds and decision rules.
6. Run or specify the test.
7. Interpret results, including alternative explanations.
8. Kill, revise, scale, or run the next discriminating test.

## Stop when

- the decision threshold is crossed;
- the test cannot distinguish among hypotheses;
- the claim has become unfalsifiable;
- the cost of additional certainty exceeds decision value.
