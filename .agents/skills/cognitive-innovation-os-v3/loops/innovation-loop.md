# Innovation Loop

**Use when:** The goal is a new product, service, campaign platform, business model, category, operating mechanism, or strategic possibility.

## Cycle

1. Define the human/system outcome and constraints.
2. Identify category conventions and hidden assumptions.
3. Generate source analogies using structural similarity.
4. Abstract the transferable mechanism.
5. Produce at least three mechanism-distinct concepts.
6. Run novelty, consumer value, feasibility, and adversarial evaluators independently.
7. Combine only complementary mechanisms; do not average away distinctiveness.
8. Convert the strongest concept into an experiment card.
9. Use results to scale, revise, recombine, or kill.

## Innovation quality gates

- meaningful user/system value;
- mechanism novelty, not surface novelty;
- comprehensibility/adoption;
- feasibility under constraints;
- defensibility or learning advantage;
- testability;
- no fatal second-order harm.

## Stop when

- one concept passes all non-negotiable thresholds;
- the portfolio contains no viable concept and the frame must change;
- two cycles produce no new mechanism or evidence;
- an external test is now the highest-value action.
