# Framing Loop

**Use when:** The mandate is vague, analysis repeatedly stalls, or different stakeholders appear to be solving different problems.

## Cycle

1. Separate desired outcome from proposed solution.
2. List observed symptoms and current problem definitions.
3. Run Wrong-Problem Detector and Assumption Archaeologist from the preserved engine.
4. Produce at least three rival problem frames.
5. Identify what each frame predicts or makes visible.
6. Seek the cheapest discriminating evidence.
7. Select, combine, or reject frames.

## Stop when

- one frame explains the important evidence better than rivals;
- the decision owner accepts a precise outcome and boundary;
- remaining ambiguity cannot be resolved without new evidence.

## Output

Problem-framing brief: outcome, competing frames, evidence, chosen working frame, falsification condition, next research/action.
