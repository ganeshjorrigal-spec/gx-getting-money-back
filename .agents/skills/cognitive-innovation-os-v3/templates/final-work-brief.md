# Final Work Brief

## 1. Mission and decision

What outcome is required, by whom, by when, and what decision is being made?

## 2. Reframed problem or opportunity

State the working frame and the strongest rival frame.

## 3. Evidence and uncertainty

Separate verified evidence, reported claims, inferences, assumptions, and unresolved unknowns.

## 4. Competing mechanisms or options

Show only structurally different alternatives and the mechanism through which each works.

## 5. Evaluation

Compare against acceptance criteria. Preserve important evaluator disagreements.

## 6. Recommended disposition

`COMMIT · STAGE · TEST · PAUSE`

Explain the decisive reason and why alternatives were not selected.

## 7. Immediate next action

Specify action, owner, timing, required input, and observable completion condition.

## 8. Risks and disconfirming conditions

What could make this wrong? What signal should trigger revision, exit, or escalation?

## 9. State and learning update

What changed in this cycle, and what should be remembered for the next one?
