# Example — Campaign Innovation Loop

## Mandate

Create a campaign for a high-awareness brand with weak trial among young urban consumers.

## Bad route

Generate taglines, influencer ideas, OOH innovations, and social content immediately.

## V3 route

1. **Mission:** increase first trial; awareness is not the outcome.
2. **Evidence gate:** awareness is verified; reason for low trial is unknown.
3. **Rival hypotheses:** perceived risk, weak occasion, low distinctiveness, distribution friction, identity mismatch.
4. **Research/test:** identify which barrier best predicts non-trial.
5. **Mechanism-distinct territories:** risk reversal; social identity permission; occasion creation; sensory demonstration.
6. **Evaluator passes:** novelty, consumer value, feasibility, adversarial review.
7. **Selection:** choose one platform and give each medium a different behavioral job.
8. **Experiment:** pre-test the mechanism, not merely creative liking.
9. **Stop:** when one territory passes value, distinctiveness, feasibility, and test thresholds.
