# Example — Product Innovation Loop

## Mandate

Improve adoption of a B2B analytics product after purchase.

## V3 route

- Reframe from “add onboarding features” to “reduce time-to-first-trusted-decision.”
- Competing causes: usability friction, data trust, organizational permission, unclear role ownership, delayed value.
- Discriminating evidence: observe first-week workflows and decisions, not only survey preferences.
- Mechanism concepts: guided decision ritual; trust/provenance layer; role-based collaborative handoff; progressive data integration.
- Evaluate value, feasibility, and adoption independently.
- Prototype the mechanism with the riskiest assumption.
- Update project state and kill/revise/scale by predeclared rules.
