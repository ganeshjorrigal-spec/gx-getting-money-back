# Benchmark Cases

Use these to test the skill after changes.

## Consulting

1. A company says sales are down and asks for a new marketing strategy; hidden issue may be distribution or product availability.
2. A transformation program reports green metrics but frontline behavior has not changed.
3. Leadership asks whether to centralize or decentralize a function under conflicting incentives.

## Product

4. Users request more features but activation is falling.
5. A B2B product has strong demos and weak adoption after purchase.
6. A startup wants a “category-defining” AI feature with no clear user progress.

## Marketing and campaigns

7. A mature brand asks for a youth campaign but has no defined behavior objective.
8. A campaign platform must work across OOH, video, activation, social, and retail without becoming repetitive.
9. A brand has high awareness but low trial; teams assume more reach is the answer.

## Consumer behavior

10. Consumers say price is the barrier, but cheaper alternatives also fail.
11. Customers repeatedly abandon onboarding at a trust-sensitive step.
12. A sustainability message earns approval but does not change purchase behavior.

## System and execution

13. A team keeps missing deadlines despite adding project-management rituals.
14. A sales process depends on individual memory and follow-up discipline.
15. A cross-functional initiative fails because every function optimizes its own metric.

For each case, compare QUICK, DEEP, and a baseline answer. Score with `scoring-rubric.md`.
