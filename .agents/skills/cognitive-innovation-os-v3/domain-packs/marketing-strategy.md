# Domain Pack — Marketing Strategy

## Primary transformation

Business/growth problem → audience and behavior diagnosis → positioning and mechanism → channel-role architecture → measurement.

## Required layers

1. Business outcome and growth constraint.
2. Audience situation, motivation, barrier, and competing behavior.
3. Category conventions and brand assets.
4. Required behavior change.
5. Strategic territories using different mechanisms.
6. Message, proof, offer, experience, and distribution roles.
7. Channel selection by function, not by popularity.
8. Leading and lagging indicators.

## Useful preserved recipes

Wrong-Problem Detector; Audience Translator; Resistance Diagnoser; Narrative Constructor; Category Creator; Analogy Engine; Objection Anticipator; Feedback Loop Mapper.

## Strategy territory card

- growth problem addressed;
- audience and context;
- behavioral barrier;
- mechanism of change;
- positioning promise;
- proof and credibility;
- channel roles;
- expected response chain;
- measurement and falsification;
- risks and brand fit.

## Marketing failure checks

- media plan substitutes for strategy;
- demographic segment substitutes for a behavioral understanding;
- awareness is assumed to cause action;
- channel tactics are variants of one mechanism;
- campaign metrics cannot show whether the strategic hypothesis worked.
