# Domain Pack — Campaign Planning

## Primary transformation

Business objective → behavior change → campaign platform → creative and media system → test and optimization plan.

## Planning sequence

1. State the commercial or organizational outcome.
2. Define the audience behavior that must change.
3. Identify the tension, trigger, barrier, and social context.
4. Audit category conventions and cultural timing.
5. Create at least three strategic territories with different mechanisms.
6. Turn the selected territory into a platform capable of multiple executions.
7. Define each medium's behavioral job: reach, credibility, salience, demonstration, trial, participation, retargeting, conversion, reinforcement.
8. Design pre-test, launch signals, and optimization rules.

## Campaign platform card

- platform idea;
- human truth/tension;
- mechanism of change;
- brand role;
- enemy or old behavior;
- proof;
- creative grammar;
- channel roles;
- activation/participation design;
- measurement chain;
- fatigue and misuse risks.

## Campaign failure checks

- a tagline masquerades as a platform;
- novelty depends only on visual spectacle;
- media is selected before the behavior and mechanism;
- all channels repeat the same message without a distinct role;
- optimization improves clicks while weakening the strategic effect.
