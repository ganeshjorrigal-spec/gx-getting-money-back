# Domain Pack — Consumer Behavior

## Primary transformation

Observed behavior → rival causal explanations → discriminating research/intervention → updated behavioral model.

## Behavioral diagnosis fields

- behavior observed;
- situation and trigger;
- current alternative or workaround;
- immediate reward/cost;
- delayed reward/cost;
- friction and ability;
- social norm and identity meaning;
- trust and perceived risk;
- memory, attention, salience, and habit;
- structural constraints and incentives.

## Hypothesis families

Do not default to “lack of awareness.” Consider:

- value is insufficient;
- value is delayed or uncertain;
- friction is too high;
- risk/trust dominates;
- social identity conflicts;
- habit/context cues support the old behavior;
- the consumer lacks ability or opportunity;
- incentives reward the opposite behavior;
- the behavior is rational under hidden constraints;
- stated preference differs from revealed preference.

## Useful preserved recipes

Differential Diagnoser; Blind Spot Finder; Assumption Archaeologist; Steelman Decoder; Resistance Diagnoser; Pattern Interrupt; Claim Verifier; Crucial Experiment.

## Deliverable

Behavioral causal map, rival hypotheses, evidence table, discriminating research question, intervention candidates, predicted effects, and decision rule.

## Failure checks

- psychologizing the consumer without situational evidence;
- using a named bias as a complete explanation;
- ignoring rational constraints;
- confusing correlation with mechanism;
- proposing an intervention that cannot distinguish among hypotheses.
