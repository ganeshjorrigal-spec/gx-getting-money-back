# Domain Pack — Product Innovation

## Primary transformation

Observed user/system struggle → opportunity mechanism → concept portfolio → validated product direction.

## Required layers

1. User situation, job, desired progress, and current workaround.
2. Behavioral and structural friction—not only stated feature requests.
3. Competing opportunity hypotheses.
4. Mechanism-distinct concepts.
5. Assumption map: desirability, usability, feasibility, viability, trust, adoption.
6. Prototype or behavioral test.
7. Product principles and staged roadmap.

## Useful preserved recipes

Blind Spot Finder; Innovation Engine; Analogy Engine; Constraint Alchemist; Category Creator; Assumption Archaeologist; Crucial Experiment; Minimum Viable Path; Downside Limiter.

## Concept card

- user and situation;
- unmet progress/tension;
- proposed mechanism;
- product behavior/experience;
- why existing alternatives fail;
- riskiest assumptions;
- smallest prototype/test;
- adoption and trust barriers;
- defensibility/learning advantage;
- kill/revise/scale criteria.

## Product failure checks

- feature novelty without behavior change;
- declared customer preference mistaken for adoption evidence;
- testing usability while ignoring value;
- roadmap before validating the mechanism;
- “AI-powered” treated as the product benefit rather than an enabling technology.
