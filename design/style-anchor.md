# Refund Genie image anchor

Medium: flat vector illustration, with simple solid shapes and restrained linework.
Palette: warm paper #f7f6f1, navy ink #14213d, amber #fde7c4, burnt orange #c2410c, white #ffffff.
Light: even soft daylight, no dramatic shadows.
Materials: matte paper and simple everyday fabric, no glossy surfaces.
Mood: grounded, patient, reassuring.
Never: 3D renders, gradients, neon, floating particles, lens flare, stock handshakes, photorealism, text or company logos inside illustrations.

Derived from the approved receipt tokens and HQ's Step 3 flat-style brief. Logo candidates are a specific exception authorised by HQ: monochrome navy marks, no model-drawn wordmark; Fraunces wordmark stays in code. Drafts are square at the model's smallest valid square size. Finals stay square, with the subject on the right and breathing room on the left. The airport board conveys a cancellation through a crossed flight symbol, without letters, following the brief's no-text rule.
